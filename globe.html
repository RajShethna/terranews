TERRA · LIVE aims to stay neutral: contested claims are attributed to each side and every story links to its original sources. Always verify specifics — especially AI-generated briefs — against those sources.
Map accuracy. The ⊞ Flat map view uses the Equal Earth projection — the equal-area projection behind NASA’s Equal Earth Wall Map (Šavrič, Patterson & Jenny, 2018). It renders every continent and country at its true relative size, so the areas shown are factually accurate — unlike Mercator-style maps that exaggerate regions near the poles.
